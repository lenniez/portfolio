2025–2026

Teachable: B2B/Enterprise Suite

Teachable had no way for businesses and educators to distribute course content to large groups of learners in a controlled, trackable way. I scoped and shipped a new B2B/Enterprise line on top of our core content tools — bulk content distribution, admin reporting on learner engagement, and new permission roles for enterprise buyers — prioritizing the seller persona first. It drove $XXXK in new revenue within 8 months of launch, with 25% early activation against a 10% benchmark and a 4/5 CSAT, becoming the foundation of Teachable's B2B strategy.

2025–2026

Teachable: AI Native Learning

Teachable needed a clear point of view on what an AI-native learning experience should feel like for students, not just for creators. I led product on a cross-functional tiger team defining that vision, then identified the highest-signal features to validate in production first: AI-generated practice exercises with new question types, personalized pre-purchase assessments, and an AI tutor that speaks in each creator's own voice.

2023–2024

Big Room

Media companies wanted to repurpose long-form video for YouTube Shorts and TikTok, but manual reformatting didn't scale. I researched, designed, and built an app from 0 to 1 that used proprietary generative AI to auto-convert landscape video into vertical formats, leading a team of 3 developers, 3 designers, and 2 QAs. It hit a 60% 30-day retention rate, with early loyal users including Hot Ones, Buzzfeed/Complex, and BratTV.

2021–2023

HIFI: Enterprise

Musicians and their business managers were stitching together royalty data from a dozen disconnected sources by hand. I built an enterprise royalty analytics dashboard from 0 to 1 — scraping and aggregating statements across 10+ services into one searchable, filterable repository — working with a squad of 7 engineers and 2 designers. It onboarded $XXXM+ in artist royalty revenue from 300+ top artists including Lizzo, Billie Eilish, and Lil Nas X, helped drive a $XXM+ Series A, and the company was later acquired by Block to become part of TIDAL.

2021–2022

Chartmetric: Dashboard, Artist Pages, Songwriters, Spotify for Artists

Chartmetric's dashboard and artist pages hadn't kept pace with how labels and managers actually used the product. I led user research and redesigned the two most-used features — the dashboard and artist pages — shipped Songwriter Pages, one of the first data sources of its kind for publishers and writers, and added a Spotify for Artists integration. The redesign landed 85% approval, and I designed the company's first company-wide KPI dashboard.

2019–2020

WeWork Apps: Home, Building Guide, Support

WeWork's member apps across iOS, Android, and web were fragmented and poorly rated by the members who relied on them daily. I led a squad of 15+ — developers, designers, a data analyst, and QA — to redesign the home tab, building guides, and support flows, and integrated Salesforce and Medallia in place of Zendesk. App Store rating climbed from 2.0 to 4.3 and Google Play from 1.5 to 3.3 within a year, serving 250K+ monthly active members.
